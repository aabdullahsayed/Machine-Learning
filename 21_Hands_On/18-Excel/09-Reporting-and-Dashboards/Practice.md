# Practice — Reporting & Dashboards

1. Take your dashboard from `05-Data-Visualization/Practice.md` and reorganize it into the standard 4-sheet structure: ReadMe, Raw Data, Calculations, Dashboard.
2. Hide the Raw Data and Calculations sheets, leaving only the Dashboard sheet visible by default.
3. Add a "Data as of: [date]" label to your dashboard, ideally referencing a formula (`=TODAY()` or a cell tracking the last refresh) rather than a hardcoded, easily-stale date.
4. Unlock any intended input/assumption cells, then apply Protect Sheet to the Dashboard — confirm you can still use slicers but can't accidentally overwrite a chart or formula cell.
5. Run File → Info → Inspect Document on your workbook and review what it finds.
6. Critique your dashboard one more time against the principles in `Report-Design-Best-Practices.md` — is there anything you'd remove, relabel, or reposition for a stakeholder seeing it for the first time?
