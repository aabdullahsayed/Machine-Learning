# Practice — Statistics in Excel

1. Enable the Analysis ToolPak, then run Descriptive Statistics on your sample dataset's Revenue column — identify the mean, median, and standard deviation, and note whether mean and median are close (symmetric distribution) or far apart (skewed).
2. Compute Q1, Q3, and the IQR for the Revenue column by hand using `QUARTILE.INC`, then identify any statistical outliers using the 1.5×IQR rule.
3. Add a second numeric column to your dataset (e.g., "Ad Spend" per region) and compute `CORREL` between it and Revenue.
4. Create a scatter chart of Ad Spend vs Revenue, add a linear trendline, and display the equation + R² on the chart.
5. Run a full linear regression via the Analysis ToolPak (Revenue as Y, Ad Spend as X) — interpret the R², the coefficient, and the p-value.
6. Try `FORECAST.LINEAR` to predict next month's revenue based on the historical trend.
7. Write 2-3 sentences explaining, in your own words, why a strong correlation between two variables in your dataset would NOT be enough, by itself, to justify a causal business claim.
