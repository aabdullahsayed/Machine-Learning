# Practice — Real-World Projects

1. Fully complete the **Sales Dashboard** project end-to-end — this should be your primary, most-polished portfolio piece.
2. Complete either the **HR Analytics** or **Financial Model** project as a second piece — pick whichever domain is more relevant to the roles you're targeting.
3. For each completed project, write a short (3-5 sentence) summary you could paste into a resume/portfolio site: what business question it answers, what tools/techniques you used, and one specific interesting insight or design decision.
4. Record a 3-5 minute screen recording walking through one of your dashboards as if presenting to a stakeholder — practicing this out loud is genuinely one of the best ways to prepare for the "walk me through a project" interview question.
5. Ask a friend/peer (ideally non-technical) to look at your dashboard for 30 seconds and tell you what they think the main takeaway is — if it doesn't match what you intended, revisit your design against `09-Reporting-and-Dashboards/Report-Design-Best-Practices.md`.

✅ Done? Move to `11-Interview-Prep`.
