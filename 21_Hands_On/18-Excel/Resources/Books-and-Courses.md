# Recommended Books, Courses & Practice Resources

## Beginner-friendly
- **Microsoft's own Excel training** (support.microsoft.com/excel) — free, always up to date with the current version's features.
- **ExcelJet.net** — an excellent free reference site with clear, example-driven explanations of formulas and features, well-suited as a companion reference alongside this roadmap.

## Structured courses
- **"Excel Skills for Business" Specialization (Coursera, Macquarie University)** — a well-regarded, structured path from basics through data analysis and dashboards.
- **Microsoft Learn's Excel/Power BI learning paths** — free, official, and particularly strong for Power Query/Power Pivot/DAX content, since it's maintained by the same team that builds the product.

## Books
- **"Excel 2019 Bible"** by Michael Alexander & Dick Kusleika — a comprehensive, deep reference covering essentially everything in this roadmap and more.
- **"Storytelling with Data"** by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic — not Excel-specific, but the definitive book on data visualization/report design principles referenced throughout `05-Data-Visualization` and `09-Reporting-and-Dashboards`.
- **"M is for Data Monkey"** by Ken Puls & Miguel Escobar — the go-to book specifically for Power Query.
- **"Supercharge Power BI"** by Matt Allington — technically about Power BI, but its DAX explanations transfer directly to Excel's Power Pivot, since they share the same underlying engine and language.

## Practice datasets
- **Kaggle Datasets** (kaggle.com/datasets) — huge variety of free, realistic datasets across every domain (sales, HR, finance, sports, etc.) — ideal raw material for the `10-Real-World-Projects` portfolio pieces.
- **Sample Superstore dataset** — a very well-known, commonly-used public dataset specifically popular for practicing sales dashboards (originally popularized by Tableau, works equally well in Excel).
- **Data.gov / data.gov.uk** — free public government datasets, good for a portfolio project with genuine real-world relevance.

## Communities
- **r/excel (Reddit)** — active community, good for troubleshooting specific formula/feature questions.
- **MrExcel.com forums** — one of the oldest, most established Excel help communities, particularly strong for advanced formula and VBA questions.
- **Exceljet, Chandoo.org** — blogs with regularly-updated tips, templates, and tutorials worth following as you continue past this roadmap.
